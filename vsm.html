Reading the result

Effective time per unit = (cycle time + changeover / batch) / uptime. Load = effective time / takt; above 100% the step cannot meet demand. Waiting time before a step = units waiting / daily demand. Lead time = total waiting days + value-adding minutes as a share of the available day. Example numbers are illustrative. Data is kept only in this browser.
