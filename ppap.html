Elements as listed in a common PPAP guide: the Design FMEA and Process FMEA are counted separately, giving 17 (some sources say "up to 18" because they split items differently). Level 3 is the usual default; Levels 2 and 4 are defined by the customer, so confirm requirements with them. Mark an element N/A only where the customer or the part makes it not applicable. This tracks status; it does not judge whether each document is adequate. Data is saved only in this browser.
