Push vs pull WIP simulator

A serial line with random process times. Compare releasing work as fast as you like (push) with capping total WIP (pull, as in CONWIP or kanban).

Throughput by WIP cap

Cycle time by WIP cap

Each run simulates 6,000 jobs with a fixed random seed, so results repeat. Process times are lognormal with the CV you choose. Push releases jobs randomly (Poisson) at the rate you set; if that rate is at or above the slowest station's capacity, queues grow without bound and the numbers keep rising with run length. Throughput gains from extra WIP flatten out at the bottleneck's capacity, while cycle time keeps climbing: the point of a WIP cap is to stop paying for WIP that buys no output. This is a simple single-product line, not a model of your plant.
