QFD: House of Quality scoring

Rate customer needs, link them to design requirements, and see which requirements deserve the most attention.

Requirement priority

Enter importance as 1 to 5 and relationship strength as 0 (none), 1 (weak), 3 (medium) or 9 (strong); this 0-1-3-9 scale is common practice. Requirement score = sum over needs of (need's share of total importance x relationship score); percent is its share of all scores. The result ranks where engineering effort best serves the customer; it does not replace testing. Competitor ratings and the roof (correlations between requirements) are left out of this scoring. Data is kept only in this browser.
