Quality Tools for Manufacturing

Browser-based calculators for the quality methods used on machining and assembly floors. Everything runs locally; your data never leaves the page.

SPC and capability

Paste measurements, get an I-MR or Xbar-R chart, Nelson rule flags, and Cp, Cpk, Pp, Ppk against your specification limits.

FMEA worksheet

Rate severity, occurrence and detection; see RPN and Action Priority side by side; export CSV.

DPMO and sigma level

Convert defects, units and opportunities to DPMO, yield and sigma level, or go back from a sigma level.

Gage R&R

Average-and-range study: repeatability, reproducibility, %GRR and distinct categories.

Pareto chart

Rank causes, see cumulative share, and find the vital few.

8D report builder

Work D0 to D8 with a rule checker for containment, escape cause and verification.

PPAP readiness tracker

Track the 17 elements by submission level and see what blocks approval.

QFD scoring

House-of-Quality arithmetic: which requirements best serve the customer.

A3 builder

Seven boxes with a checker for the structural misses: no numbers, solutions before analysis, no follow-up date.

Changeover (SMED) analyser

Split tasks into machine-stopped and machine-running, see the reduction, and what it does to batch size.

Kaizen sustainment tracker

Track whether improvements still hold months later. Flags gains that decayed and ones nobody re-checked.

OEE, takt and flow

OEE with loss breakdown, takt and line balance, Little's Law, and queue time from utilization.

Push vs pull WIP simulator

See how a WIP cap trades throughput against cycle time on a line with random process times.

Value stream map builder

Current-state lead time, value-added ratio and load against takt for each step.

Framework selector

Tick your symptoms, get the frameworks most likely to help, with reasons.

Read before relying on results

  • Capability indices assume a stable, roughly normal process. Check the control chart first; a Cpk from an unstable process describes nothing.
  • Cp and Cpk use the within-subgroup sigma estimated from the chart; Pp and Ppk use the overall sample standard deviation.
  • The Action Priority column is an illustrative severity-weighted rule, not the AIAG-VDA table. Load a table you are licensed to use to get official values.
  • The A3 checker tests structure, not the quality of the thinking. It cannot tell a real root cause from a plausible one.
  • Changeover case figures in the literature are nearly all consultancy self-reports with no baselines; measure your own rather than planning to a published percentage.
  • The kaizen tracker's readiness prompts restate the four factors from Glover's study; they are discussion prompts, not his validated survey instrument.
  • Gage R&R here uses the average-and-range method (2 to 3 appraisers and trials); it ignores appraiser-by-part interaction. The PPAP and 8D tools track status and rules, not document quality.
  • The WIP simulator is a simple single-product serial line with a fixed seed; use it to build intuition, not to size your plant.
  • Sigma level uses the conventional 1.5 shift. Some organisations report without it, so state which convention you use.
