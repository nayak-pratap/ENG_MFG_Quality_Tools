Signals

Signals use Nelson rules 1 to 3: a point beyond 3 sigma, nine in a row on one side of the centre line, six in a row rising or falling. Cp and Cpk use within-subgroup sigma; Pp and Ppk use the overall standard deviation. Expected PPM assumes a normal distribution.
