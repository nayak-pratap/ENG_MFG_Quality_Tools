Kaizen sustainment tracker

Logging improvements is the easy half. This tracks whether each one still holds months later, and flags the two ways gains disappear: decay, and nobody re-checking.

Before you run a kaizen event: readiness

Score each from 1 (not at all) to 5 (strongly). These restate the four factors that predicted sustainability in Glover's study of 65 events. They are discussion prompts, not his validated survey instrument, so use them to start the conversation, not to produce a number for a report.

How status is decided. Direction is inferred: if your target is below the baseline, lower is better. Gain is measured against the baseline, and expressed as a percent of the target gain. Holding means the latest check is at or above your threshold. Decayed means it reached the threshold at some point and has since fallen below it. Short means it never got there. Unverified means nobody has checked since the change was made, which is the state the research points at: organizations often see promising early results, then find performance back near pre-event levels within six to twelve months because no one looked.
