Gage R&R (average and range)

Enter the readings from a crossed study. The tool separates measurement error from real part variation.

Share of total variation

Method: average and range, with constants 1/d2* from the standard MSA tables (2 to 3 appraisers, 2 to 3 trials, 2 to 10 parts). Repeatability (EV) is the average within-appraiser range times K1; reproducibility (AV) comes from the spread of appraiser averages, corrected for EV; part variation (PV) from the spread of part averages. %GRR = GRR / total variation. Common criteria (automotive practice): under 10% acceptable; 10 to 30% depends on application and cost; over 30% not acceptable; number of distinct categories should be at least 5. Always use parts that span the real process range, randomise the order, and make appraisers unaware of earlier readings. This method ignores appraiser-by-part interaction; use an ANOVA study when that matters.
